# Checkout Queue Management & Staffing Escalation Policy

## 1. Queue Benchmarks & SLA Targets
- **Optimal Queue Length**: 0 to 2 customers per active billing counter.
- **Congestion Threshold**: 4 or more customers in line, OR estimated wait time exceeding **3.5 minutes**.
- **Customer Abandonment Risk**: Industry telemetry proves retail shoppers abandon purchases if checkout wait exceeds 5 minutes. Every walkout represents an average basket loss of ₹350–₹500.

## 2. Escalation & Counter Dispatch Protocol
- **Level 1 (Normal Operations - 1 to 3 Shoppers)**:
  - 1 Billing Counter active.
  - POS cashier processes transactions.
  - Floor associate focuses on shelf replenishment.
- **Level 2 (Congestion Alert - 4+ Shoppers or >3.5 min wait)**:
  - Audio/Visual alert triggered on SmartRetail Edge Dashboard.
  - Floor associate is immediately dispatched to open **Counter 2 (Express Checkout)** within 90 seconds.
  - Express checkout accepts UPI / Card transactions (maximum 5 items) to rapidly flush the queue.
- **Level 3 (Severe Bottleneck - 7+ Shoppers or >6 min wait)**:
  - Store Manager intervenes to assist with item bagging and scanning.
  - Temporary queuing stanchions deployed to maintain orderly flow.

## 3. Customer-to-Staff Ratio Guidelines
- **Target Staffing Ratio**: 1 staff member per 4 to 6 active store shoppers.
- If the customer-to-staff ratio exceeds 8:1 during peak hours (e.g., 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM), staff must suspend secondary back-office tasks and focus strictly on customer assistance and checkout velocity.

# Retail Loss Prevention, Shrinkage & Financial ROI Guidelines

## 1. Edge AI Business Impact & ROI Measurement
- **Revenue Protected Metric**:
  - Quantified as the total revenue saved by preventing stockouts through automated predictive alerts and preventing queue abandonment.
  - Calculation formula: `Sum of (SKU Price * Units Restocked Before Complete Depletion) + (Queue Customers Saved from Walkout * Average Basket Size ₹350)`.
- **Labor Efficiency Gains**:
  - Continuous computer vision shelf audits save approximately 3 to 4 staff hours per day otherwise spent on manual clipboard auditing.
- **Stockout Reduction Target**:
  - Target: Maintain >30% reduction in shelf stockouts compared to non-monitored retail baselines.

## 2. Planogram Violations & Shrinkage Mitigation
- **Misplacement as Leading Indicator**: Items repeatedly abandoned in non-home shelf zones indicate customer purchase hesitation or intentional concealment.
- **Immediate Action**: Return misplaced high-value items (such as Pringles ₹110 or Dairy Milk ₹50) to designated displays immediately.
- **Cryptographic Audit Ledger**: Every critical incident (price mismatch, sustained stockout > 30 minutes, or severe checkout congestion) is immutably logged to the local cryptographic hashgraph ledger for end-of-day store manager audits.

## 3. Shoplifting, Theft Prevention & Security Protocol
- **CCTV Computer Vision Dwell Alerts**: When a shopper dwells in high-shrink zones (e.g. Chocolates or Personal Care) for >4 minutes without interacting with checkout, CCTV flags a subtle staff advisory.
- **Non-Confrontational Customer Service Deterrence**: Floor associates are instructed to use proactive customer service ("Namaste! May I help you find what you need today?"). Visible staff presence deters 92% of casual pilferage without escalating disputes.
- **Concealment Procedure**: If active item concealment is suspected, staff must NEVER physically restrain the individual. Alert store manager immediately and cross-reference checkout transaction logs before the individual exits.
- **High-Value Item Audits**: High-value items (Pringles ₹110, Colgate ₹55, Dairy Milk ₹50) must undergo hourly automated vision headcount to detect bulk sweeps.

## 4. Sales Revenue Growth & Commercial Strategy
- **Protect High-Margin Driver SKUs**: Monitored products with >35% gross margins (Oreo 40%, Lays 40%, Coca Cola 37.5%, Fanta 37.1%) must receive priority eye-level shelf space and zero stockout tolerance.
- **Eliminate Queue Abandonment**: Customers abandon baskets when queue wait exceeds 3.5 minutes. Dispatching Counter 2 within 90 seconds recovers an estimated ₹12,000 to ₹18,000 in monthly retained revenue.
- **Cross-Merchandising Synergy**: Pair impulse snack categories (Lays and Pringles) directly alongside chilled beverage coolers (Coke and Fanta) to lift multi-item basket size.
- **Price Integrity**: Ensure EasyOCR shelf tag prices match POS ERP scanner prices exactly to prevent checkout disputes and customer drop-offs.
